## One team, many pathologies

Our research is not tied to a single disease. A given imaging instrument or tool can be useful in many situations, so we work across embryonic development and the lung, liver, heart and vessels, brain, ear-nose-throat, joints, skin, digestive tract and mouth.

Translational research is a pillar of the Institute for Advanced Biosciences, and the proximity of the university hospital lets us start from real clinical needs. Clinicians are part of the team and several of our tools have already reached the hospital.

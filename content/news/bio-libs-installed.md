---
title: "Bio-LIBS, the first LIBS microscope in a hospital, is installed"
date: 2024-06-15
summary: "A unique elemental microscope for biomedical samples is now running at Grenoble Alpes University Hospital."
---

In June 2024, the Bio-LIBS system was installed at the CHU Grenoble Alpes. It is the first laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy microscope dedicated to biomedical samples to reach a clinical environment.

It was financed by regional, national and university funders, and it supports a first clinical study using LIBS imaging on lung tissue. Read more about the [Bio-LIBS platform](../../platforms/bio-libs/).

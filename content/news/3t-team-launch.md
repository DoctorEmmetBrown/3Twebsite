---
title: "The Translational Theranostics Team starts in January 2027"
date: 2026-11-02
summary: "Imaging physics, elemental imaging and theranostics join forces in a single team at the Institute for Advanced Biosciences."
---

In January 2027, the Translational Theranostics Team (3T) begins its activity at the Institute for Advanced Biosciences, under the co-leadership of Lucie Sancey (CNRS) and Emmanuel Brun (Inserm).

The team gathers three groups, **Metals in Medicine**, **X-Ray Wave Imaging for Health** and **Image-guided Surgery and Neutron Capture Therapy**, around a common aim: better understand pathologies and their treatments, and bring new instruments and compounds from the laboratory to the clinic.

---
title: "arXimed: bringing phase-contrast X-ray imaging to dental clinics"
date: 2025-02-15
summary: "The arXimed project, supported by the SATT Linksium, takes wave-based X-ray imaging toward clinical systems for oral healthcare."
---

In February 2025, the arXimed project received new support from the SATT Linksium to continue the transfer of phase-contrast and dark-field X-ray imaging toward clinical systems, starting with oral healthcare.

The project builds on the ANR ROOTS project and on the MUSITOX platform. See [how the approach works](../../research/xray-wave-imaging/).

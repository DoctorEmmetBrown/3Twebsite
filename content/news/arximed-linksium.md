---
title: "arXimed: bringing phase-contrast X-ray imaging to  clinics"
date: 2026-10-30
summary: "The arXimed project, supported by the SATT Linksium, takes wave-based X-ray imaging toward clinical systems for radiology."
---

In October 2026, the arXimed project received new support from the SATT Linksium to continue the transfer of phase-contrast and dark-field X-ray imaging toward clinical systems.
Read more on  [the arXimed website](https://www.arximed.fr)
The project builds on the ANR ROOTS project and on the MUSITOX platform. See [how the approach works](../../research/xray-wave-imaging/).

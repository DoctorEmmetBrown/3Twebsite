keywords:
  - Translational investigations

objectives:
  - title: Understand disease mechanisms
    text: Tell physiology from pathology, and follow the response to treatment, notably through the role of metals in health.
  - title: Develop imaging instruments and tools
    text: New instruments and contrast agents, validated for preclinical and clinical use, from 3D imaging of biopsies to optimised tumour surgery.
  - title: Design theranostic compounds
    text: Targeted nanovectors and drugs that combine imaging, optical or X-ray, with remote activation by neutrons, light or enzymes, to reinforce the cancer arsenal.

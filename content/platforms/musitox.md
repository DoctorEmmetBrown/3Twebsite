MUSITOX brings to the laboratory what, until now, was only possible at a synchrotron: seeing soft tissue and sub-resolution structure in 3D, without staining and without destroying the sample.

## What it does

- **Wave-based imaging.** Beyond absorption, the platform measures refraction (phase contrast) and small-angle scattering (dark-field), which give access to soft tissue and to microstructure far below the pixel size.
- **Multi-resolution.** It is designed to image the same sample at several resolutions at once, from the whole specimen to its fine details.
- **Beam modulation.** A single, easy-to-manufacture membrane structures the X-ray beam, which considerably simplifies dark-field imaging in the laboratory.

## What it is for

- 3D virtual histology of biopsies and organs.
- Testing our methods on real human cases outside the synchrotron.
- Development of high-energy phase-contrast and dark-field CT for oral healthcare, in the ANR ROOTS project, and transfer toward clinical systems with arXimed.
